Getting a stripped-down Doom clone running on a TI-84. Because someone has to keep the tradition alive.

* * *

### Status

Currently running at about 4 frames per second, which is technically playable if you're patient and enjoy pain. Rendering is the bottleneck — no surprise there.

* * *

### Next steps

- Precompute more of the wall-casting math instead of doing it live
- Cut the resolution down further
- Accept that this was never going to be a good idea and keep going anyway
